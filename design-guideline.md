### **Quick Reference: Color Combinations**

**Perfect Pairings with Maroon:**

* ✅ **Maroon + Warm Ivory** - Classic romantic contrast (couple against backdrop)
* ✅ **Maroon + Champagne** - Soft, luxurious harmony (attire coordination)
* ✅ **Maroon + Terracotta Blush** - Warm, earthy romance (florals)
* ✅ **Maroon + Dusty Rose** - Tonal sophistication (family attire)
* ✅ **Maroon + Natural Oak** - Grounded elegance (venue integration)

**Avoid These Combinations:**

* ❌ Maroon + Pure White (too stark, clinical)
* ❌ Maroon + Bright Colors (clashes, overwhelming)
* ❌ Maroon + Cool Greys (wrong temperature, dull)
* ❌ Maroon + Black (too heavy, formal, gothic)

**Color Ratios for Balanced Decor:**

* **60%** Warm Neutrals (Ivory, Champagne, Sand) - The foundation
* **25%** Earth Accents (Taupe, Terracotta, Sage) - The texture
* **10%** Maroon Echoes (Burgundy florals, ribbons) - The romance
* **5%** Deep Accents (Burgundy Brown details) - The depth

**Lighting Temperature:**

* Use **2700K-3000K** (Warm White/Soft White) for all lighting
* Avoid **4000K+** (Cool White/Daylight) - will wash out warm tones
* **Amber uplighting** enhances maroon richness
* **Golden hour simulation** creates romantic atmosphere

## 3. Recommended Typography

We need a font pairing that screams "Modern" but remains romantic.

### **Primary Typeface (Headings & Initials)**

* **Font:** **Playfair Display** (Serif) or **Tenor Sans** (Sans Serif with character).
* *Why:* It is editorial, clean, and easy to read from a distance.
* *Usage:* "The Wedding of", Section signs (Buffet, Welcome).

### **Secondary Typeface (Body Text)**

* **Font:** **Montserrat** or **Lato** (Light / Regular weight).
* *Why:* Geometric and modern. Easy to read on menus/programs.
* *Usage:* Menu lists, directional signage text, souvenir cards.

### **Accent Typeface (Names/Signatures)**

* **Font:** **Pinyon Script** or **Mrs Saint Delafield**.
* *Why:* A thin, refined script that isn't too "loopy" or old-fashioned.
* *Usage:* Athur & Dara's names on the invitation or backdrop.

## 10. Do's and Don'ts for Vendors

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use **matte finishes** (doff) on all materials | Use shiny gold, glitter, or metallic finishes |
| Use **warm-toned metals** (brushed gold, copper) | Use silver, chrome, or cool-toned metals |
| Use **amber/warm glass** and natural ceramics | Use bright white ceramics or colored glass |
| Embrace **natural wood tones** (oak, walnut) | Use dark stained wood or painted wood |
| Keep **asymmetric, organic** arrangements | Create perfectly symmetrical or round arrangements |
| Use **warm lighting** (amber, golden) | Use cool white or blue-toned lighting |
| Layer **textures** (linen, silk, dried florals) | Use synthetic fabrics or plastic elements |
| Keep **negative space** - let it breathe | Overcrowd tables or backdrops |
| Use **elegant serif & clean sans-serif** fonts | Use chunky, decorative, or script-heavy fonts |
| **Echo maroon** subtly in florals (burgundy dahlias) | Use maroon in large quantities (competes with couple) |

### **Color Temperature Rule**

Everything should lean **WARM**:

* Whites → Warm Ivory (not cool white)
* Greys → Warm Taupe (not cool grey)
* Metals → Gold/Copper (not silver)
* Lighting → Amber/Golden (not daylight/cool)
* Greens → Sage/Olive (not bright/emerald)
